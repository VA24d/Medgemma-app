Pervasive AI: Context and Task Specification
1. What “Pervasive AI” Means
Pervasive AI is AI that is embedded into many devices and environments so that intelligence is available everywhere, not just in a central cloud service.

It builds on the idea of pervasive (ubiquitous) computing: sensors, connectivity, and compute are integrated into everyday objects, running continuously in the background and reacting to context with minimal user intervention.

In a pervasive AI system, decisions and learning can happen at multiple layers: on-device, at the edge (near the data), and in the cloud, forming an “edge–cloud continuum.”

The goal is to choose where each workload runs so that the overall system is fast, reliable, cost‑effective, privacy‑aware, and scalable.

For Qualcomm’s SparQ theme “Pervasive AI,” there is a strong emphasis on AI inference and intelligence happening on devices and at the edge, with the cloud used where it adds value at scale.
